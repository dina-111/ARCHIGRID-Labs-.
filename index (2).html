How a remote project runs

Everything online

  1. Brief

    You send plans, photos, measurements and references. The brief builder below helps you do it.

  2. Concept

    A moodboard and layout direction to react to, shared as images and short notes.

  3. 3D renders

    The chosen direction is modelled and rendered so you can see the space as built.

  4. Revisions

    You comment on the images. Changes are made until the design is right.

  5. Delivery

    Final renders and drawings arrive by file transfer, ready for your builder or supplier.

Table démontable

Location
France
Type
Custom furniture, 2026
Scope
Design, technical drawings, 3D render

A round outdoor table in two rigid halves that sits around a planter without touching it. Each half stands on its own, and the legs come off by hand so the table can be stored flat.

Concept

Two rigid halves, not foldable, placed around the planter with no contact or load on it. Round top 2200 mm across around a 1000 mm planter, 20 mm thick, 720 mm total height.

Materials

Top in a honey-yellow wood plank, protected with marine varnish or epoxy powder coat. Legs in stainless steel, 35 × 35 mm, adjustable.

Legs

Eight removable legs, four per half. Each pulls out by hand once its pin is released, with no tools, and stores flat at 635 mm long.

Drawings

Drawings are indicative and in French. Dimensions are in millimetres and are to be validated by the workshop before fabrication.
